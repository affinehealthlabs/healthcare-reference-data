# **Privacy Policy**

*Last Updated: 4/1/2025*

Affine Health Inc. (“we”, “us”, or “our”) is committed to protecting the privacy and security of health data processed through the **Affine Data Platform** (“Platform”). This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard Protected Health Information (PHI) and other sensitive data in compliance with applicable privacy regulations, including the **Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act of 1996 (HIPAA)**, the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), and other relevant laws.

---

## **1\. Scope**

This Privacy Policy applies to all data processed through the Affine Data Platform by healthcare organizations, payers, providers, care management teams, researchers, and authorized users.

---

## **2\. Information We Collect**

We may collect or process the following types of information:

* **Protected Health Information (PHI)**: As defined by HIPAA, this may include claims, clinical data, member identifiers, diagnoses, procedures, lab results, and enrollment information.

* **Operational metadata**: Logs, system usage metrics, access history, and job performance.

* **User access credentials and audit trails**: Collected to ensure compliance and traceability.

---

## **3\. HIPAA Compliance**

Affine Health Inc. is fully committed to compliance with the **Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act (HIPAA)** and the **HITECH Act**, including the Privacy, Security, and Breach Notification Rules.

As a **Business Associate (BA)**, we:

* Enter into **Business Associate Agreements (BAAs)** with Covered Entities or other Business Associates.

* Limit access to PHI based on **least privilege and role-based access**.

* Use **end-to-end encryption** (TLS 1.2+, AES-256) to protect PHI in transit and at rest.

* Maintain **HIPAA-compliant cloud infrastructure** (AWS HIPAA-eligible services).

* Implement **audit logging**, **intrusion detection**, and **data loss prevention (DLP)** tools.

* Conduct regular **risk assessments**, **penetration testing**, and **employee HIPAA training**.

Clients retain control over their PHI, and Affine will only process data as instructed, with strict adherence to the applicable BAA and HIPAA obligations.

---

## **4\. Purpose of Data Use**

The Affine Data Platform standardizes, analyzes, and makes healthcare data actionable for:

* Risk adjustment and predictive modeling

* Quality measurement (e.g., HEDIS, Stars)

* Care gap identification and clinical pathway support

* Population health analytics

* Regulatory and contractual reporting (e.g., VBC programs)

---

## **5\. Legal Basis for Processing**

Data is processed:

* Under **Business Associate Agreements (BAAs)** for HIPAA-covered entities

* Under **Data Processing Agreements (DPAs)** for non-U.S. jurisdictions

* As required to fulfill contractual obligations and comply with legal requirements

---

## **6\. Data Security Measures**

Affine employs a layered security model that includes:

* **Encryption** of all data at rest and in transit

* **MFA and RBAC** for user authentication

* **Secure VPC deployments** with private subnets and firewalls

* **SOC 2 Type II** controls across systems and environments

* **Automated compliance monitoring**

Affine does **not store or process PHI outside of secure, HIPAA-compliant environments**.

---

## **7\. Data Retention & Deletion**

All PHI and client data are retained only for the duration necessary to fulfill the stated purpose or as contractually agreed. Upon request or contract termination, Affine will securely delete or return all PHI in compliance with HIPAA and client directives.

---

## **8\. Data Sharing**

We do **not sell, rent, or commercialize PHI**. Data may be shared only with:

* Authorized personnel under client access controls

* Sub-processors under HIPAA-compliant agreements

* Legal or regulatory authorities when required by law

A list of sub-processors is available on request and included in the BAA.

---

## **9\. User Rights and Access Controls**

We support Covered Entities and clients in fulfilling obligations related to:

* Access and amendment of PHI

* Restriction of use

* Audit trails and accounting of disclosures

* Breach notification and incident response

All user actions are logged and monitored.

---

## **10\. Changes to This Policy**

This policy may be updated to reflect changes in regulations, services, or practices. All updates will be posted at https://affine-health.io/privacy and communicated to clients directly where required.
